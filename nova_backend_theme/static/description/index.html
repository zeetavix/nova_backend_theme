Nova Backend Theme

A calm, accessible, white-label backend for Odoo 20 Community

Your accent · Your logo · Light and dark, designed separately

Lists that stay calm

Tabular numerals, readable status pills and one accent reserved for actions. Density, corner radius and an opt-in column spotlight adapt to each user, with a company default behind them.

Forms that read like paper records

A warm paper canvas, boxed inputs that show their bounds, and a statusbar that reads at a glance. The same rhythm carries into kanban, calendar, activity, pivot, graph and Discuss.

A real dark theme

Charcoal surfaces with elevation by lightness, desaturated state colours and re-tinted charts. Per user from the user menu, or follows the operating system.

Your client's identity on the login page

The company's own logo, name and contact email beside a calm sign-in card. The same identity drives the browser tab icon and document title. Wide wordmarks are laid out as-is, never squashed.

Appearance and branding in Settings

Six accent presets or any brand hex — hover, active, soft tints and the readable foreground are derived automatically, so even a light accent keeps its button text legible. The branding block previews the logo and favicon that are actually live.

What you get

One accent, yours
Presets or a custom hex, used consistently in the backend, the portal and the login page.

White-label branding
Brand name, contact email, login logo and favicon per company — oversized uploads downscaled, dark logos plated, favicons squared.
