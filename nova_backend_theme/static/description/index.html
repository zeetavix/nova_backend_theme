Nova Backend Theme

A calm, accessible, white-label backend for Odoo 20 Community

One teal accent, soft geometry and Inter typography across every view. A real dark theme. Branding per company. Appearance controls per user. Nothing is forked: every change is an additive inherit, so removing the module brings stock Odoo back.

Designed as one system, not a skin

Every surface was rebuilt against the same rules: a single action colour, layered radii, quiet shadows, tabular numerals and motion that respects reduced-motion. Light and dark are designed separately, never inverted. Lists stay calm and scannable, with tabular amounts, readable status pills and one accent reserved for actions.

Forms that read like paper records

A warm paper canvas, boxed inputs that show their bounds, and a statusbar that reads at a glance. The same rhythm carries into every other view type.

A real dark theme

Charcoal surfaces with elevation by lightness, desaturated state colours and re-tinted charts. Toggle it per user from the user menu, or let it follow the operating system.

Your client's identity on the login page

The login page is a branded split shell: the company's own logo, name and contact email on the left, a calm sign-in card on the right. The same identity drives the browser tab icon and the document title. Wide wordmark logos are laid out as-is, never squashed.

Appearance and branding in Settings

Accent: six presets or any brand hex, with hover, active, soft tints and the readable foreground derived automatically. Density, corner radius and an opt-in column spotlight each have a company default and a per-user override. The branding block previews the logo and favicon that are actually live.

One language across every view

What you get

One accent, yours
Presets or a custom hex, with derived hover, active, soft and readable foreground tones used consistently in the backend, the portal and the login page.

White-label branding
Brand name, contact email, login logo and favicon per company. Oversized uploads are downscaled, dark logos get a light plate, favicons are squared automatically.

Comfort controls
Density, corners and column spotlight, per user with a company default.

Accessible by default
Visible focus rings, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion and forced-colors support, contrast-checked accents.

No forks
Additive template inherits only. Uninstall and stock Odoo returns.

Self-hosted type
Inter is bundled, so no request leaves your server for a font.
