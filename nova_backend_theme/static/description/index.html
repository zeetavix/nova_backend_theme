Start the day knowing exactly where you stand

Overdue work up top. One-tap quick create. Live numbers — open invoices, waiting quotations, active tasks — before you click anything.

Lists that stay calm at 500 rows

Tabular numerals. Status pills you read at a glance. Dashboard cards that summarise before you scroll.

Forms that read like paper records

A warm paper canvas. Inputs that show their bounds. A statusbar with real contrast.

One design language, every view

Pipeline, schedule, margins — same pills, same cards, same calm.

A real dark theme, not an inverted one

Charcoal surfaces with elevation by lightness. Per user from the avatar menu — or follows the operating system.

Your brand, everywhere your client looks

Six accent presets or any brand hex. White-label login page, browser tab and document titles — set once in Settings › Appearance.

Live in three steps

1. Install. Drop the nova_backend_theme folder in your addons path, update the apps list, install. Only dependency is web.

2. Tune. Settings › Appearance — accent or brand hex, density, corners, column spotlight.

3. Brand. Name, contact, login logo and favicon. The login page, tab icon and titles follow automatically.

Why teams keep it

✓ Accessible by default — focus rings, 44px targets, reduced-motion, forced-colors.

✓ No forks, no lock-in — additive inherits only; uninstall returns stock Odoo.

✓ Self-hosted type — bundled Inter, zero outside requests. A handful of SCSS files and two small JS modules.
